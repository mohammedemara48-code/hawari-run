# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-09

### Changed

- Completely redrawn, larger بلبل with an expressive face, three-tone clothing, satchel and crisp one-pixel contours.
- Added 46 small/powered character drawings, eight-frame running, breathing/blinking, skid, rise/fall, crouch, hurt/death, growth and throwing.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-09

### Added

- Two original Egyptian platforming worlds, secret treasure room and checkpoint lanterns.
- Original pixel artwork, animated characters, parallax scenery and synthesized soundtrack.
- Growth, chili projectiles, invulnerability, stompable enemies and kickable baskets.
- Arabic RTL screens, detailed level results, keyboard/touch controls and offline installation.
